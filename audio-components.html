Sound pads .pad

One-shot and hold pads are buttons (hold lights while pressed); toggle and loop pads are checkboxes, so they latch with no script. --progress fills the bottom bar; the app plays the sound and binds the key.

Waveform regions and markers

Drag a region's edges or its body; each edge is a native range input in seconds, so arrow keys nudge it by one step, Page Up and Page Down jump a tenth of the clip, and screen readers hear its time. Marker buttons seek; hover shows the time under the pointer. assets/js/controls.js keeps it all in step.

Fades, ghost and zoom

A cue's trim with a fade in and a fade out. Each bar's --gain is the fade's gain at that moment, so the waveform shows what will be heard; the ghost shows what the fade removes. Drag the trim edges or a fade's free edge. Zoom with the buttons, the mouse wheel to pan, a box drag, or the window in the overview: no input is ever clamped.

How the pieces fit

For a console, start with .mixer and .strip; for playback, compose .tracklist, .waveform, .scrubber and .transport. Add your engine separately and feed its values into the markup. The audio integration guide maps every AudioCN component and its hooks to an existing class or an app-owned responsibility.
