Collaboration and notifications

Presence, live cursors, comments, mentions, the notification centre and the activity feed (PLAN.md §19). Apps send the live data; these classes style every state. Nothing on this page needs JavaScript: the bell, the pins and the avatar stack open popovers, the drawer is a popover, and the tabs and toggles are radios and checkboxes.

Presence .presence

An avatar stack with a “+3” overflow. As a button it opens who's viewing; as a group it is just a picture with a label. Status dots differ in shape as well as colour.
