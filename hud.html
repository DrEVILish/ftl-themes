Game HUD

Resource bars, minimap, quest tracker, hotbar and inventory, floating numbers, achievement toast and key prompts, laid over a scene. Corners on wide screens, stacked edges on phones, and the middle of the screen always clear. Try it in Skyrim, Pip-Boy, XMB and Cassette Futurism.

HUD overlay .hud

Six regions over a .hud-scene (any image, SVG, video or canvas). The middle row stays empty; only floating numbers cross it.

Quest tracker .hud-quests

Objectives are an <ol class="hud-objectives">: done, current (aria-current="step"), failed and pending each have their own mark, with an optional .hud-count.

Hotbar and inventory slots .hud-slot

Square slots with an icon, key, count and a cooldown sweep from --cooldown (0–1). Rarity from data-rarity sets the border, glow and 1–5 pips. A hidden radio selects the slot.

Inventory grid .hud-inventory

Floating numbers .hud-float

Placed at --x/--y in the nearest positioned box. With motion on they rise and fade once; here they repeat. With motion off they stay put until the app removes them.

Achievement toast .toast.hud-achievement

A core .toast with an icon, title, description, score and optional progress. Put it in a .toast-region with popover="manual" so it shows over everything, even an open modal.

Key prompts .hud-prompt

Core <kbd> for keys; kbd.hud-pad for gamepad buttons, ringed by data-button (south, east, west, north). A <button class="hud-prompt"> is the tap target on touch screens.
