FAQ .faq

Questions and answers, two columns when there is room. For a collapsible list, use .accordion.

How do I pay?
By card, or by invoice on annual plans. Prices include VAT where it applies.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. Upgrades apply now and are prorated; downgrades apply at the next renewal.
Is there a free trial?
Every plan has 14 days free, no card needed.
Where is my data stored?
In the region you pick at sign-up: EU, US or Asia-Pacific.
