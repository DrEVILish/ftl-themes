Music and media

The v5 media kit (PLAN.md §19), built for Playlist Lab: track rows, album grid, the now-playing bar (docked at the bottom of this page), queue, waveform scrubber, shuffle/repeat/like toggles, lyrics and a mini player. Built on core's .transport, .btn-go, .knob and the image card.

Track rows .tracklist > .track

Number, art, title and artist, album, like, duration and a row menu. The number turns into a play button on hover or keyboard focus (on touch the number is the button). Row 3 is playing (animated equaliser, motion settings permitting), row 5 is selected (.is-active), rows 4 and 7 are explicit, liked rows keep their heart. The album column hides in a container under 480px.

The live one is this page's footer: in the shell's status slot, stuck to the bottom of the screen, clear of the home indicator. Desktop: art and title, transport over the scrubber, extras with volume. Tablet: the scrubber takes its own row and only the mute button remains. Phone: play and next only. Shuffle, repeat, like and volume .media-toggle

On is the accent plus a dot (a filled heart for like), never colour alone, and nothing changes width. Repeat cycles off, all, one with three radios and no script (click, or arrow keys).
