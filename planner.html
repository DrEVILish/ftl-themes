Planner

The productivity kit: a kanban board, a calendar with month, week and day views, a Gantt timeline and a schedule list. Apps move cards, place events and draw connectors; ftl-themes styles every state. Today is Friday 2 October 2026, 13:36.

Kanban board

Columns with a count and WIP limit (“In progress” is over it), cards with labels, priority, due dates (soon, overdue) and assignees. “Review” is the drop target while a card is dragged, with its drop placeholder. “Blocked” is empty. On phones the board scrolls sideways one column at a time.
