Metering

Analogue VU and PPM needles, LED bargraphs, arc loudness meters, vacuum-fluorescent displays and an EQ with RTA and spectrogram overlay. Every meter is driven by --value / --peak; the gentle movement here is CSS keyframes (.is-demo-live) and stops under reduced motion.

Analogue VU .vu

Labelled arc scale from list markup, red zone from 0 VU, peak LED, a needle with 300 ms VU ballistics. Cream paper face in light themes, the theme's own face in dark ones.

LED bargraph .ledbar

Dual column with a centre scale, a red reference band, ghosted unlit segments, peak hold and an overs cap.

Horizontal bargraph .is-horizontal

The same contract laid across, with a shared scale under it. Right has tripped its overs cap.
