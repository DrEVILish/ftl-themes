# steampunk — reference design notes

## What steampunk is

A retro-futuristic subgenre of science fiction and fantasy imagining an
alternative history in which modern technology runs on 19th-century
industrial steam machinery rather than electricity, electronics or
petroleum — "a future that never was". It blends the elegance of the
Victorian and Edwardian eras (or the American Wild West) with advanced,
anachronistic inventions.

## Core aesthetics and technology

Steampunk technology is mechanical, visceral and complex.

- **Power sources:** steam engines, clockwork mechanisms, gears and cogs,
  early electrical experiments such as Tesla coils.
- **Inventions:** brass-plated airships (zeppelins), clockwork automatons,
  mechanical ray guns, analogue mechanical computers in the manner of
  Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine.
- **Materials:** raw industrial textures — polished brass, copper, wrought
  iron, riveted steel, dark wood, polished leather.

## Materials palette

- **Metals:** aged brass, polished copper, cast iron, bronze, weathered steel.
- **Textiles:** heavy velvet, brocade, lace, satin, distressed leather, tweed.
- **Hardware:** exposed cogs, mechanical gears, rivets, compasses, pocket
  watches, brass pipe fittings.

## Interface components and widgets

- **Gauges and dials:** CPU, memory and network readings become analogue
  pressure gauges, mercury thermometers, voltmeters or astrolabes with
  needle indicators.
- **Cogs and gears:** background elements or loading spinners are
  interlocking brass gears that physically rotate or pulse while a
  process runs.
- **Tubes and valves:** vacuum tubes, glowing amber/green Edison-style
  filaments and copper pipe conduits represent data streams or power
  status.
- **Switches and levers:** toggles and buttons become heavy knife switches,
  rotary dial knobs, or spring-loaded brass push-buttons that depress with
  a visual (or audible) mechanical click.
- **Skeuomorphism with a twist:** elements mimic real materials and weight,
  but must stay clear enough to read at a glance.
- **Lighting and glow:** warm amber, soft gaslight yellow or emerald
  vacuum-tube glow marks active states against dark, sooty backdrops.

## How the `steampunk` theme maps this

Valve handwheels for knobs, sight glasses for faders/progress/sliders,
glowing brass push-buttons for keys, brass nameplates for titles, copper
steam pipes and a turning gear train in the shell, parchment for the EQ
trace. Nixie tubes for counters and timecode, edgewise needle gauges for
horizontal meters, knife switches for toggles and filigree corners on cards
and dialogs were added in the second pass.
