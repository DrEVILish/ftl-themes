# Cassette Futurism: research notes

Palette, shape and type guidance only. Every image here is a real photograph
of period hardware from Wikimedia Commons under a free licence (credits
below); no film stills, logos or artwork are reproduced. Research done
2026-10-01 by web search plus the Commons API. The Aesthetics Wiki page
returned HTTP 402 to a direct fetch, so its definition rests on the search
summary.

## What the genre is (sources)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Retro-futurism of roughly the early 1970s to mid-1990s: early microcomputers, late-Cold-War tech, 1970s-80s sci-fi imagery and interiors. | Aesthetics Wiki, "Cassette Futurism" https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cassette_Futurism (search summary) |
| Plastic housings in beige or soft cream; CRT screens with a warm glow in green, amber or pale blue; chunky beige computers, bulky casings. | Same; TV Tropes "Cassette Futurism" https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/CassetteFuturism (summary) |
| Ron Cobb's Nostromo interiors for *Alien* (1979) are the touchstone: functional, industrial, "every surface built to be gripped and operated", nothing decorative. | designbycurio.com https://designbycurio.com/learn/cassette-futurism-1979 ; Alien Explorations, "Ron Cobb's design philosophy" http://alienexplorations.blogspot.com/1979/05/ron-cobbs-design-philosophy.html (summaries) |
| Angular plastic with hard edges, single-function buttons; Dieter Rams (Braun) as an influence; white/beige/black. | martin-fieber.de https://martin-fieber.de/blog/cassette-futurism/ (summary) |

## Typography

- **Numerals:** seven-segment (LED clocks, HP-35, Technics counter) and VFD
  segments. Vendored **DSEG7 Classic Bold Italic** (keshikan, SIL OFL 1.1),
  limited to digits and `: . -` by `unicode-range`.
- **Panel lettering:** silk-screened Helvetica/Univers-class sans, caps,
  tracked (TR-808 legends, Marc receiver). System stack, nothing vendored.
- **Terminal text:** monospace in the dark windows; uses the repo's
  vendored Share Tech Mono.
- **Dymo:** heavy embossed capitals on coloured tape, as CSS text-shadow on
  tape-coloured badges.

## Gaps

No period reference was found for: tables, modals/dialogs, toasts/alerts,
tabs, dropdown/context menus, pagination, progress bars as such, message
bubbles. Those are extrapolated from the housing/keycap/window language. The
Nostromo set itself is not represented by a free-licensed image; it informs
the mood only.
