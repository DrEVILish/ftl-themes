# lcars.org.uk panel captures

Every LCARS example on http://www.lcars.org.uk/ (the `lcars.htm` page plus its TNG, TNG-films and DS9 panel galleries), captured one image each in headless Chromium after the image had fully loaded and decoded (animated GIFs held 2.5 s so the animation is under way; the two MP4s on `lcars.htm` captured at 3 s and 6 s). Converted to WebP. Source images are screen-used Okudagram recreations collected by the site; credit lcars.org.uk.

The Voyager gallery is a Flash animation and could not be captured.
