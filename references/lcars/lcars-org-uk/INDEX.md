# lcars.org.uk panel captures

Every example on http://www.lcars.org.uk/ (the `lcars.htm` page plus its TNG, TNG-films, DS9, Alien, Enterprise, TOS and Star Trek (2009) panel galleries), captured one image each in headless Chromium after the image had fully loaded and decoded (animated GIFs held 2.5 s so the animation is under way; the two MP4s on `lcars.htm` captured at 3 s and 6 s). Converted to WebP. Source images are screen-used Okudagram recreations collected by the site; credit lcars.org.uk.

The Voyager gallery is a Flash animation and could not be captured. Site decorations repeated across gallery pages (the UFP red-alert emblem, nav animations) were left out.

Only the TNG, TNG-films, DS9 and `lcars.htm` captures are LCARS proper. The Alien (Klingon, Romulan, Cardassian, Ferengi, Bajoran and other species), Enterprise (NX-01, pre-LCARS), TOS / TOS-films and Star Trek (2009) galleries are other Trek interface languages: references for contrast, or for a future theme, not for the `lcars` theme.
