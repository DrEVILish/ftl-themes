# barbie — visual reference notes

Outsider read: Brand pink Pantone 219C + gold sparkle, rounded glam wordmark, pill chrome.

Capture targets: Dreamhouse box; logo; official app UI.

Sources: Shades of pink Wikipedia.

Use side-by-side with example.html; local captures are copyrighted originals — this file is the textual checklist.
