## Rules taken from the frames

- **Panel = bracket + tab.** A 3px rounded bracket on the left (the ends
  curve inward), open on the right, with a filled pill tab carrying the
  name at the top left. Fill is a slightly lighter navy glass.
- **Palette:** navy-black glass `#03111b`–`#0b3550`; hologram cyan
  `#4fd2ff`; teal `#3ee8c4` for second series and toggles; amber
  `#ffb51f` for call-outs (and the whole UI in the suit room); orange
  `#ff8a1f` for edge codes; magenta and violet only inside plots.
- **Type:** wide squared capitals, light weight, tracked, for every label;
  small squared mono for the numbers. Michroma, Share Tech and Share Tech
  Mono are the OFL stand-ins.
- **Values:** small dark-blue boxes with glowing digits, units in muted
  text after them.
- **Background:** a regular grid of short dashes with sparser crosses.
- **Edges:** orange vertical code strings up the left margin; vertical
  rotated tabs on the right edge (not reproduced: they need markup).
